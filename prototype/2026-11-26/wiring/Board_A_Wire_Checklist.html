First check on USB power

  1. Plug both Feathers into the computer and keep the motor supply off. On the micro-USB board the screen should show Calibration. Turn the knob and watch the selected action change. Choose STOP motor to check Select, then press Back. Keep the wheel empty.
  2. Open the micro-USB board’s Serial Monitor at 115200 baud, select Newline and send STATUS. Expect Link=online, phase=0, fault=0, configuredMask=0x01 and sensor=online after a moment. The scale’s rawHX reading should change if you add a small test weight.
  3. If the RTC reads unset or shows the wrong time, send TIME YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS using your current local date and 24-hour time. Send STATUS again and check RTC=set and the local time.
  4. With the bowl installed and empty, send TARE. Wait for Empty bowl zero recorded. Add a known mass and send CAL 10.00 only if it weighs 10.00 g. Substitute its actual mass otherwise. Keep the total load, including the bowl, within 100 g.
  5. After calibration succeeds, remove the reference mass. Add the lightest inert piece you plan to test and send VERIFY. Remove it after the success message and send STATUS to check that the empty bowl reads near 0 g.
